# Softwarebois Reimagined — Reference Specification

The supplied screenshots are the **ground-truth layout and content specification**. The final site will retain their long-form, single-page community archive structure: sticky light navigation, dramatic Ganesh hero, compact event ribbon, annual idol gallery, immersion albums, winners cards, auction story, group photograph, member roster, UPI support, location/contact cards, feedback form, and compact footer.

## Chosen Approach: Indigo Saffron Archive

### Design Movement
Contemporary Indian editorial design: a quiet archival layout with festival photography as the visual spectacle, expressed through disciplined spacing and small ceremonial details rather than ornate UI decoration.

### Core Principles
1. Preserve the visual rhythm and section ordering of the reference, including its generously spaced reading column and card compositions.
2. Recast the reference palette through deep indigo, saffron, lotus pink, parchment, and antique gold—high contrast but warmer and more distinctive than the original teal-and-rust treatment.
3. Replace the serif/sans pairing with Fraunces for expressive display text and DM Sans for compact, highly readable metadata and controls.
4. Let photography remain vivid and immersive; interface surfaces stay restrained, translucent, and lightly textured.

### Color Philosophy
Parchment creates the calm, archival ground. Deep indigo provides spiritual depth and stabilizes the colorful festival imagery. Saffron delivers ritual warmth for actions and emphasis, while lotus pink is used sparingly for social and celebratory accents. Antique gold acts as the quiet connective thread in borders, labels, and dividers.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is a vertically unfolding community ledger. A left-weighted editorial column drives the narrative, while recurring card groups, short bands, and generous section dividers create a tactile record of annual celebrations rather than a conventional dashboard.

### Signature Elements
- Indigo rounded-square section seals containing minimal line icons.
- A thin antique-gold rule between major archival chapters.
- Low-opacity paper grain and subtle gradient wash over parchment surfaces.

### Interaction Philosophy
Navigation scrolls directly to named chapters. Cards lift gently on hover, the member strip scrolls horizontally with native controls, and interactive controls provide immediate tactile feedback without interrupting reading.

### Animation
Use a restrained 180–240ms custom ease-out for card lift, buttons, and the sticky navigation transition. Section content may fade upward subtly when it first enters the viewport, with motion disabled for users who request reduced motion.

### Typography System
Headlines use Fraunces, especially medium and semibold weights, with selective italic emphasis in the countdown phrase. DM Sans carries body copy, data labels, and navigation at compact sizes with clear tracking. Headline scale remains generous in the hero and intentionally modest inside archive sections.

### Brand Essence
**An evolving Ganesh Chaturthi memory-book for Rampuram’s youth group—designed to keep every celebration visible, collective, and close to home.**

Personality: **warm, rooted, celebratory**.

### Brand Voice
The voice is specific, communal, and quietly proud; it speaks as a group preserving its own history rather than as a generic event platform.

Example headline: “Every year, a new idol. Every year, the same us.”

Example CTA: “Open this year’s album”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact indigo circular seal with a gold line-art Ganesha-inspired abstract mark, paired with a slanted Fraunces wordmark. The mark is intentionally symbolic, uses no text, and is legible at navigation scale.

### Signature Brand Color
**Festival Indigo — #26275E**.

## Style Decisions

- Saffron is reserved for primary ritual actions and the featured annual record; secondary archive actions use Festival Indigo or parchment with antique-gold edges.
- Every chapter retains a visible archive cue through its seal, warm gold divider, ledger-like metadata, or memory-book caption.
- Copy speaks as the local group preserving its own history: “Open the 2025 memories” and “Leave a note in the ledger” replace generic platform language.
